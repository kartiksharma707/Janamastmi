About the Festival

Significance

Krishna Janmashtami, is a Hindu festival celebrating the birth of Lord Krishna,Marks the birth of Lord Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu. Observed with fasting, bhajans, and midnight aarti.

How to Celebrate
  • particularly in Mathura and Vrindavan, where Krishna spent his childhood
  • Decorate with peacock feathers & flutes
  • Sing bhajans & read scriptures
  • Attend midnight aarti
Dress Code

Popular choices include bright colors like yellow, red, and green, often seen in sarees, lehengas, salwar kameez, or kurta sets. Avoid black, as it is considered inauspicious for the occasion.
